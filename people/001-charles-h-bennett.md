# Charles H. Bennett

## Identity

Charles H. Bennett is an American physicist and information theorist associated with IBM Research.

He is known for foundational work in:

- quantum information science
- quantum cryptography
- reversible computation
- quantum teleportation
- BB84 with Gilles Brassard

He shared the 2025 ACM A.M. Turing Award with Gilles Brassard.

Do not confuse him with Charles Henry Bennett, the nineteenth-century British illustrator.
